## Embidextrous 0.2.0

A new Home screen, per-phone profiles, a Bedtime button, and the new Hall Monitor name and look. "Watching Eye" is now **Hall Monitor** everywhere in the app (its server plugin was renamed too).

### What changed
- **Home screen.** The app opens on a dashboard: a status line, what is being watched now, the kids' time today with week totals, and a Bedtime card.
- **Profiles per phone.** Pick *Parent* (Hall Monitor only), *Administrator* (everything) or *Custom* (Hall Monitor, with Smart Lists switched on if you want it). Change it any time in Settings. A regular account is always Parent; an administrator is asked once.
- **Bedtime.** One tap puts the kids on a time-out for "1 hour" or "until 6:00 AM", with Undo, and "End time-outs" to lift them early.
- **Time left now rounds up.** After adding 15 minutes it shows 15, not 14.
- **Hall Monitor look.** New deep violet, violet and mint palette as the default theme (your accent colour choices are still there), a new app icon (two circles with a list, text bars and a play button), and the Hall Monitor name on the widget and notifications.
- **Alerts and widget.** Limit alerts with add-time buttons (refused while the phone is locked), a home-screen widget, and an option to hide names in the widget.
- **Kids' week totals** on their cards.

### Upgrading
Install over 0.1.2 (same app, same signing key); your sign-in and settings are kept. Obtainium picks it up by itself. After updating, enter the Hall Monitor key again if the app asks, and choose a profile.

### Verify your download
```
gh attestation verify Embidextrous-0.2.0.apk --repo feerlessleadr/embidextrous
```

### Known limits
- Checked on an emulator against a test server only; not yet on a real phone: the notification icon in a real notification, very large font sizes, the Custom profile on a device, Home with a regular (non-administrator) account, starting and undoing "until 6:00 AM" Bedtime, and the 5-minute retry after a wrong key.
- Back from the Smart Lists or Settings tab goes to the Home tab first.
- A list opened through "Import a shared list" is not treated as changed, so leaving it without saving gives no warning.
- Needs Hall Monitor plugin 1.9 or newer (1.10 carries the new name).
