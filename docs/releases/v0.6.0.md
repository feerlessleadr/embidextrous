## Embidextrous 0.6.0

Adds a Hall Pass tab. Install over 0.5.1; same app, same signing key. The new tab needs the **Hall Pass plugin 1.1.0.0 or newer** (released at the same time); everything else works as before.

### What's new
- **Library → Hall Pass.** Review the music tracks the Hall Pass plugin could not decide, from your phone. A summary card shows the counts and scan progress, with Scan now, Scan all again and Apply. Filter by Needs review, Explicit, Likely clean, Clean or All, search by artist, album or title, and mark a track Explicit or Clean (tap a card to see why Hall Pass was unsure, or clear your decision).
- **Albums view.** Group the list by album and mark a whole album explicit or clean in one tap (it asks first and covers every track of the album, not just the ones on screen).
- **Apply** shows what would change and only offers to write the tags when dry run is off. The plugin's settings (word list, online lookups, album and artist tags, dry run) stay on its Emby page on purpose.
- The tab uses the signed-in administrator's account; no extra key. It says so when the plugin is missing or too old.

### Hall Pass plugin 1.1.0.0 (released with this)
Word-list editing, an optional lyrics fallback, a Try it tab, "Rescan this track", and optional album and artist tags. See its release notes.

### Known limits
- The Hall Pass tab was checked on an emulator against the test server only: not on a real phone, not with Apply while dry run is off, not with a large library, and the not-installed and too-old screens were not seen.
- Everything listed under 0.4.0 to 0.5.1 still applies.
