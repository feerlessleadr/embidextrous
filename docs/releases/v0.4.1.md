## Embidextrous 0.4.1

A small update on top of 0.4.0. Install over it; same app, same signing key.

### What's changed
- **Server → Live shows the show's poster for an episode**, not the episode's thumbnail. Movies and other items keep their own picture. If Emby has no poster for the show, no picture is shown.

### Verify your download
```
gh attestation verify Embidextrous-0.4.1.apk --repo feerlessleadr/embidextrous
```

### Known limits
- Everything listed under 0.4.0 still applies (not yet used on real phones, not checked against the real Tracearr).
- Checked on an emulator only with a test show that has no poster of its own, so you will see the absence of the thumbnail there; the choice of the show's picture is covered by a unit test. Live was seen to lag for about a minute once on the emulator; the cause is not known.
